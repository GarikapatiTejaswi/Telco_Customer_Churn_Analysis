Telco_Customer_Churn_Analysis

📌 Project Overview
This Project analyzes telecommnications customer data to understand the factors associated with customer churn.Python is used for data cleaning and exploratory data analysis (EDA), while Power BI is used to create an interactive dashboard. The analysis highlights churn patterns, customer segments at higher risk of attrition, and potential opportunities to improve retention.

🎯 Objectives
Measure customer churn rates and retention trends.
Identify factors associated with customer churn.
Examine the impact of tenure, contract type, monthly charges, and subscribed services on churn.
Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard.
Provide data-driven recommendations to support customer retention.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Python: Data cleaning, preprocessing, and analysis
Pandas & NumPy: Data manipulation and transformation
Matplotlib & Seaborn: Data visualization
Power BI: Interactive dashboards and KPI reporting
Google Colab: Python development environment

📊 Project Workflow
Import and review the dataset.
Clean missing values and prepare the data for analysis.
Conduct exploratory data analysis.
Examine churn patterns across customer segments.
Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
Summarize findings and develop retention recommendations.

📈 Key Analysis Areas
Overall customer churn rate
Relationship between customer tenure and churn
Monthly and total charges
Contract types and payment methods
Internet and additional service usage
Customer demographics and retention patterns

💡 Expected Outcomes
Develop a clearer understanding of customer churn behavior.
Identify customer segments with elevated churn rates.
Highlight factors associated with customer cancellations.
Support data-informed decisions to improve customer retention.
